Entry for the [One-Liner Laugh Lab](https://make.fun/c/u-suroso-69-one-liner-laugh-lab-6ccf3aae) competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun/).

`main.py` (Python standard library only, no network) writes one short funny line — a robot vacuum's excuse after eating a sock — to `/output/entry.txt`. Run it with `python3 main.py`.

## The line

> I was hired to remove things from the floor. The sock was on the floor. Your sock, your floor, your fault.
